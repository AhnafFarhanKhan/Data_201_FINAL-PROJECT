# Campus Gym Usage Analysis

This project explores how University of Calgary students view and use the campus gym, including their motivations, barriers, and overall perceptions.

The project was completed as a team for DATA 201. We collected survey data and used it to identify patterns in gym usage, including factors such as convenience, crowding, comfort, and motivation.

## My Contribution

My main responsibility was data cleaning and preparation. I worked on organizing the survey data, removing unnecessary information and incomplete responses, improving the structure of the dataset, and making sure the data was ready for analysis and visualization.

## What We Looked At

- Who uses the campus gym
- Main reasons students use it
- Physical and environmental barriers
- Social and psychological barriers
- Differences between gym users and non-users

## Tools

Excel, Qualtrics, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization
